# 〜たい (Want to do)

> [!NOTE] Formation Rule
> Take a verb in polite form, drop `ます`, and append `たい`:
> - 食べます → 食べ**たい** (want to eat)
> - 行きます → 行き**たい** (want to go)
> - 見ます → 見**たい** (want to watch)

---

### ⚠️ Pitfall
> [!WARNING] First-person Only
> `〜たい` describes private internal feelings. Saying `クリスさんは行きたいです` sounds like you claim to read Chris's mind! Use `行きたがっています` for others.

---

### 📖 Examples
- 日本に行き**たい**です。 (I want to go to Japan.)
- ラーメンを[[食べる|食べたい]]です。 (I want to eat ramen.)
