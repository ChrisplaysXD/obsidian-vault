# Particle は (wa) vs が (ga)

> [!NOTE] Cure Dolly Model
> - **は (Topic marker)**: Sets the stage. Translates to: *"As for X..."* or *"Speaking of X..."*. Everything following は is the actual new information (the comment).
> - **が (Subject identifier)**: Pinpoints the specific actor. Translates to: *"It is X (and none other) that does the action."*

---

### ⚠️ Pitfall & Real-World Nuance
> [!WARNING] The Spotlight Analogy
> In `猫は魚を食べる` (Cats eat fish), the statement is a general fact about cats.  
> In `猫が魚を食べた` (THE cat ate the fish), the spotlight is pointing at the cat that did the crime!

---

### 📖 Example Sentences
- 私はクリスです。 (As for me, I am Chris.)
- だれ**が**来ましたか？ (WHO was it that came?)
- 田中さん**が**来ました。 (It was Tanaka who came.)
