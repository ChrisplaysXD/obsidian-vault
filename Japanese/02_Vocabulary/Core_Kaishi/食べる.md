# 食べる (たべる)

**Meaning**: to eat  
**Part of Speech**: Ichidan Verb (Ru-verb)  
**JLPT**: N5  

---

### 🎯 Context & Core Conjugations
- **Polite (Present)**: 食べます (tabemasu - eat / will eat)
- **Polite (Negative)**: 食べません (tabemasen - do not eat)
- **Polite (Past)**: 食べました (tabemashita - ate)
- **Te-form**: 食べて (tabete)
- **Desire Form**: [[Tai_form_desire|食べたい]] (tabetai - want to eat)

---

### 例文 (Example Sentences)
> 朝ごはんを**食べた**。  
> (I ate breakfast.)

> 何を**食べます**か？  
> (What will you eat?)
